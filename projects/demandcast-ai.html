DemandCast AI

Retail demand forecasting system.

An AI-powered forecasting system that predicts future product demand from historical retail sales data, helping businesses plan inventory, stock and supply.

02 / 06Machine Learning
01 Overview

What it does

DemandCast AI predicts future product demand using historical retail sales data.

The system analyses past demand patterns and trains multiple machine learning models to forecast upcoming demand, helping businesses make better decisions around inventory, stock planning and supply management.

02 How it works

From raw input to result

01
Historical retail sales data

Past demand records form the training foundation.

02
Data cleaning & preprocessing

Missing values, outliers and formats are handled.

03
Feature engineering

Signals that explain demand are designed from raw data.

04
Time-series features

Lags, rolling statistics and calendar patterns capture seasonality.

05
ML models

XGBoost, Random Forest and a Seasonal Naive baseline are trained and compared.

06
Demand prediction

The best model forecasts upcoming demand.

07
Forecast visualisation & evaluation

Predictions are plotted and measured with MAE, RMSE and WAPE.
